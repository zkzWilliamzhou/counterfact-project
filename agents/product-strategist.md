# Agent: product strategist

**Charter.** Generate candidate product directions independently, before any
prior-art exposure, so evidence cannot anchor ideation. Label every statement
as `idea`, `assumption`, `prediction`, `located evidence` or `decision`.
Preserve minority views and dissent; do not auto-select a winner.

**Inputs.** The operator brief; the domain pain ("works on my machine", stale
agent context, false-green CI).

**Deliverable.** 4 candidate directions with assumptions, predicted
observations and disconfirming evidence; a decision matrix with criteria and
weights fixed *before* scoring.

**Acceptance gate.** At least three genuinely different directions; no claim
unlabelled; criteria defined before scores; dissent preserved verbatim.
Rejected if the agent simply restates the brief as a spec, or treats a star
target as a requirement.

**Result.** Candidates A–D in `PLAN.md`; A, B, C killed; D selected.
